## Mathematical audit

### Adopted theorem

Let

\[
P_n=F_{n+1}F_{n+2},
\qquad
X_A=2^{12(2^{A+1}-1)}.
\]

The Binet decomposition is correct:

\[
P_n=
\frac{\varphi^{2n+3}}5
+
\frac{(-1)^n-\varphi^{-(2n+3)}}5
=:L_n+\rho_n.
\]

The correction bound is correct:

\[
|\rho_n|<\frac14
\quad(n\ge0).
\]

The equality obstruction is correct. Consecutive Fibonacci numbers are coprime. If their product is a power of two, each factor is a power of two and one factor must be one. For the indexed products `P_n`, this leaves only `P_0=1` and `P_1=2`. Since every QBL threshold satisfies `X_A>=4096`,

\[
P_n\ne X_A
\quad(A,n\ge0).
\]

Thus `P_n-X_A` is a nonzero integer and

\[
|P_n-X_A|\ge1.
\]

Since `P_n=L_n+rho_n` and `|rho_n|<1/4`, the exact and leading differences have the same sign, with

\[
|L_n-X_A|>\frac34.
\]

By the definition

\[
y_A=
\frac{12(2^{A+1}-1)\log2+\log5}{2\log\varphi}
-\frac32,
\]

we have

\[
L_n>X_A\iff n>y_A.
\]

The same integer-gap argument excludes `y_A` from the integers. Therefore

\[
T_A
=\min\{n:P_n\ge X_A\}
=\min\{n:n>y_A\}
=\lceil y_A\rceil
\]

for every `A>=0`.

The global bridge is therefore proved. No Baker–Matveev cutoff is needed.

## Defects requiring p5-b2-v3

### 1. Returned archive hash is wrong

This is the blocking delivery defect. The final hash must be computed from the exact archive returned to the user, after all packaging steps have finished.

### 2. The archive itself is not generated by the documented builder

The builder regenerates the extracted package root and manifest, but it does not create the final ZIP or print its SHA-256. The reported archive hash therefore cannot be reproduced from the package's documented command.

### 3. Several notebook PASS labels exceed the checks

- The Binet cell checks twelve integer samples. The universal identity is proved in the document, not by that cell.
- The correction cell samples forty values. The universal bound is proved in the document, not by that cell.
- The equality-obstruction cell scans thirty products. It does not prove the universal power-of-two classification.
- The integer-gap cell checks a finite grid of examples. It does not prove the abstract lemma.

These cells are useful regression tests, but their headings and PASS status should distinguish `SYMBOLIC/FORMAL PROOF` from `FINITE REGRESSION CHECK`.

### 4. The derivation script does not independently establish the theorem

It scans the correction for 100 values and the power-of-two obstruction for 100 products, then emits a hard-coded global `PROVED` status. The hand proof is valid, but the script should encode and assert the actual proof obligations rather than use finite scans as universal certificates.

### 5. The Lean surface is incomplete

The included Lean file contains generic integer-gap and separation lemmas. It does not formalize:

- the exact Binet product identity;
- the global correction bound;
- the consecutive-Fibonacci power-of-two obstruction;
- nonintegrality of `y_A`;
- the final global threshold theorem.

Lean is unavailable, so compilation is correctly not claimed. The source should still state the full theorem surface before Branch 2 is archived.

### 6. One comparison phrase is too strong

The document calls the direct logarithmic lower bound “stronger” than a Matveev bound. The direct integer-gap route is simpler and sufficient here. A numerical or asymptotic comparison proving that it is stronger is not supplied. Replace “stronger” with “direct and sufficient.”

## Final disposition

The mathematical result is accepted. Branch 2 should not advance until the exact returned archive, reproducible ZIP builder, reported hash, and theorem companions are synchronized in `p5-b2-v3`.
