# Intrinsic Action Equivalence

Two complete presentations are equivalent only through a prefix-indexed representation isomorphism preserving:

- custody state and exact word;
- the custody-selected primitive;
- all three transition case maps;
- the four descendant placements;
- active and latched sectors;
- old/new extension structure;
- seed and all prefixes;
- no-projection evolution.

Formally, transition conjugacy and descendant commutation must hold at every prefix.

The exact admissible representation/gauge category remains open. Therefore transpose-based and conjugate-transpose-based candidates have not been proved inequivalent or equivalent.
