# Intrinsic Action Equivalence After Factorization Repair

Two presentations represent the same intrinsic action only when there is a prefix-indexed family of representation isomorphisms that preserves all retained structure.

For every exact word prefix `W_t`, the comparison must commute with:

1. the custody state and custody-only primitive selector;
2. exact word retention;
3. ambient and chart argument objects;
4. `iota_+` and `iota_-` placements;
5. the primary pairing and its codomain evaluation;
6. the realization-fixed non-vacuous restriction constructor;
7. all four ordered descendants;
8. each `B`, `Q`, and `L` case map;
9. the old/new sector embeddings at every `L`;
10. the primary seed and every reachable prefix;
11. the prohibition on projection during evolution.

A pullback presentation, regeneration presentation, and direct value-update presentation are coordinate-equivalent only after such a commuting family is exhibited. Agreement on the local scalar descendant or on operation counts is insufficient.
