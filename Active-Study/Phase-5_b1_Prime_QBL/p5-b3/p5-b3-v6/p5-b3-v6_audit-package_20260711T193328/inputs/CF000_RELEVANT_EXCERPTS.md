# CF000 Relevant Excerpts and Audit Use

Source: `CF000_Primitive_Distinguishability.pdf`.

## Section 4.2, Higher-Resolution Degree Character Lemma

A genuinely higher-resolution degree adds an internal determination rather than a vacuous restatement. The lower degree is no longer sufficient as the sole determination.

Audit use: CF000 requires genuine added articulation. It does not define this as information-theoretic independence from the complete set of lower-level paths and all functions of those paths.

## Sections 4.6 and 4.7, irreducibility and saturation

After same-axis saturation, further genuine articulation requires a new axis or domain irreducible to the exhausted one. A purported same-axis continuation is either genuine novelty showing the axis was not saturated, or mere renaming or reparameterization.

Audit use: irreducibility must be established. CF000 does not equate irreducibility with a split fiber of an artificially completed state-and-path functor.

## Sections 4.8A and 5.2, effective-invariant stacking

Each newly admitted domain inherits the primitive invariant and prior effective-invariant stack, then adds an effective invariant proper to that domain. The effective invariant may be a new relational or organizational law native to that domain.

Audit use: deterministic derivability from inherited structure is not by itself a disqualifier. Otherwise CF000's own effective invariants would be excluded by an information-independence rule not stated in the source.
