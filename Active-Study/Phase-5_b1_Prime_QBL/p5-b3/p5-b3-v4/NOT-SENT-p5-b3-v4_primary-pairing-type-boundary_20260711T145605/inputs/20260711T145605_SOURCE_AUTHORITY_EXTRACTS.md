# Existing-authority extracts for the primary-pairing type boundary

**Purpose:** compact source evidence used by the derivation script. The complete primary law remains the controlling source.

## Current primary law

The current law states that:

- `P_t` is generated by the retained state and exact ordered word;
- both chart restrictions and both directed transfers derive from one `P_t`;
- `B` and `Q` preserve pairing rank;
- `L` preserves the old pairing block, appends one new active orthogonal axis, and increases rank by one;
- the formulas involving `iota_+^* P_t iota_+` and mixed pullbacks are explicitly described as an **intended algebraic shape**;
- the exact all-depth primary-pairing recurrence, chart maps, and transfer recurrences remain open;
- a single diagonal lens is prohibited as a substitute for the dual-chart pairing-first object.

## Downstream Hermitian geometry

CF00 derives a Hermitian covariance only after smooth quotient descent and removal of vertical phase motion. It states that the QGT is downstream quotient geometry, not a primitive input to the retained QBL grammar. It therefore cannot silently choose the type of `P_t`.

## Quarter-turn carrier

CF13 supplies a real two-axis carrier and the quarter-turn operator `J`, with complex interpretation as multiplication by `i`. It supplies the local orientation witness but does not define the primary Orthad pairing.

## Downstream FQM polarization

The current law defines the polarization of a finite quadratic module as bilinear only after the Orthad-to-FQM bridge. That downstream bilinear form does not identify the earlier generative primary pairing.

## Older diagonal Orthad material

Older Orthad documents use a diagonal matrix shorthand and initialize a new active entry by `1`. The current law classifies a single diagonal lens as incomplete and forbids it as a replacement for the dual-chart pairing-first object. These documents are provenance only for this pass.
