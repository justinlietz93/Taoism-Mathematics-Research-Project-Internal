## Research result

The weakest exact interface forced by the pairing-first architecture is:

[
P_t:H_t\rightarrow D(H_t),
]

where (D) is a contravariant duality. Every chart or transfer block has the common form

[
D(\iota_a)\circ P_t\circ\iota_b.
]

This types both chart restrictions, both directed transfers, and the gauge action

[
P_t\mapsto D(U)\circ P_t\circ U
]

without prematurely selecting a bilinear, sesquilinear, or Hermitian realization.

The concrete type remains open because the sources do not determine whether scalar action obeys

[
P_t(\lambda x,y)=\lambda P_t(x,y)
]

or

[
P_t(\lambda x,y)=\lambda^{*}P_t(x,y).
]

Hermitianity is a further self-adjointness condition inside the conjugate-dual branch. Historical (H=M+iJ) supplies downstream Hermitian type evidence, but not the clean seed or recurrence.

At the first `L`, old-block retention plus the stated two-sided orthogonality forces the mixed birth blocks to vanish:

[
P_{t+1}=
\begin{pmatrix}
P_t&0\
0&p_{\mathrm{new}}
\end{pmatrix}.
]

The new-axis value (p_{\mathrm{new}}) remains underived.
