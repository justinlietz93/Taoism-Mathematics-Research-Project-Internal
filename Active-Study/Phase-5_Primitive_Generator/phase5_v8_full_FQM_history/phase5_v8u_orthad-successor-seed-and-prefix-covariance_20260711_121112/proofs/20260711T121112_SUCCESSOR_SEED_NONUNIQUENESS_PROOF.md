# Successor-seed nonuniqueness on the supported Domain-0 surface

## Proposition 1

For `C = Z/12Z`, the translation `T_a(x)=x+a` is a single 12-cycle exactly when `gcd(a,12)=1`.

### Proof

The orbit of `0` under `T_a` is the cyclic subgroup generated by `a`. Its order is `12/gcd(a,12)`. Therefore the orbit contains all 12 elements exactly when `gcd(a,12)=1`. The admissible increments are `1,5,7,11`. Hence the carrier and the single-cycle property alone do not select `T_1`. ∎

## Proposition 2

Every 12-cycle on a 12-element set is conjugate to `T_1` on `Z/12Z`.

### Proof

Choose one point as `0` and label its successive images under the cycle as `1,...,11`. In that coordinate the cycle is `x -> x+1`. Changing the chosen origin or orientation changes the coordinate presentation without changing the abstract cycle. ∎

## Consequence

`x -> x+1 mod 12` is a valid coordinate normal form for a chosen 12-cycle. It is not forced as the clean QBL-native seed until the clean retained state supplies an address/orientation map into `Z/12Z` and the first-`B` covariance square.
