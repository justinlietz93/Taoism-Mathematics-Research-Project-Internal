# Ambient spectral module role

The linear layer is required to state eigenvectors, eigenforms, Fourier kernels, and the successor-to-pairing bridge. It is not a custody primitive and it is not the earliest operational gap.

For a 12-cycle, the minimum splitting field over the rationals is `Q(zeta_12)`. A natural representation is the group algebra `K[Z/12Z]` or `Fun(Z/12Z,K)`. Unitary Fourier normalization additionally requires `1/sqrt(12)`; `C` is a sufficient ambient field.

This typing does not derive the clean successor or pairing.
