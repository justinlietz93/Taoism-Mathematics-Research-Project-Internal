# Ambient module role

Status: `OPTIONAL_FORMAL_PRESENTATION`.

The retained finite product carrier and point-index basis are concrete. One may linearize the carrier as K[C] to discuss eigenvectors and bilinear forms. The source set does not canonically choose K, and the linearization is not a new custody primitive.

Therefore the ambient module functor is useful formal machinery, but it is not the earliest gap. The earliest operational gap is the B/Q/L successor update on the concrete carrier.
