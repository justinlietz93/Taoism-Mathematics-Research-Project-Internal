# Successor-to-pairing bridge

The fixed cyclic shift s_D(x)=x+1 has the forced character eigenvectors chi_b(x)=exp(2*pi*i*b*x/D). This is rederived exactly for D=12 and D=24.

The canon phrase `pairing = eigenbasis/eigenform of the native successor` does not specify a well-typed equation. A pairing is a bilinear or sesquilinear map; an eigenbasis is a basis. The sources do not decide among:

1. evaluation pairing P(delta_x,chi_b)=chi_b(x);
2. a Gram form diagonalized by the character basis;
3. the normalized Fourier kernel as an operator;
4. a word-dependent deformation of one of these.

Hence the primary pairing recurrence remains open even after the fixed-factor successor clue is recovered.
