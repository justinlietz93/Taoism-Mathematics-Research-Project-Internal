# Bilinear Underdetermination Proof

Let `H = Q^2`, `C+ = Q`, `C- = Q`, with embeddings

\[
\iota_+(x)=(x,0),\qquad \iota_-(y)=(0,y).
\]

Define two symmetric bilinear forms by matrices

\[
P_0=egin{pmatrix}1&0\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
P_1=egin{pmatrix}1&1\1&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]

For all `x,y in Q`, both restrictions are identical:

\[
P_0(\iota_+x,\iota_+y)=xy=P_1(\iota_+x,\iota_+y),
\]

and

\[
P_0(\iota_-x,\iota_-y)=xy=P_1(\iota_-x,\iota_-y).
\]

But the mixed values differ:

\[
P_0(\iota_-1,\iota_+1)=0,\qquad
P_1(\iota_-1,\iota_+1)=1.
\]

Therefore fixed chart modules, fixed embeddings, symmetry, and both diagonal restrictions do not determine the mixed transfer block. This theorem is conditional on an atlas already being typed. In the actual source corpus, the gap is earlier because that atlas is not yet defined.
