# Conditional Pipelines

These constructions remain useful when their inputs are supplied explicitly. They may become canonical after a clean pairing-to-transition provenance theorem.

## Transition and cocycle layer

- v7m trace/cocycle normal form;
- v7p support-derived event alphabet;
- v7q transition assignment;
- v8a confluence and cocycle engine.

Condition: the event alphabet and transition records must be regenerated from the current `P_t`, chart restrictions, and transfers.

## Transition-to-FQM layer

- v7t transition-record to FQM extraction;
- v7u full lens-compiler binding;
- v7r/v7s classification and normalization tools;
- v8d family-F presentation and later exact classifiers.

Condition: no package may call its FQM an Orthad output until the complete provenance chain is machine-checkable.

## Projection/readout layer

- v7w parity seating;
- v7x depth channel readout;
- v7z finite `chi12` skeleton.

Condition: these are expected-output tests. They cannot choose, repair, or infer missing retained structure.
